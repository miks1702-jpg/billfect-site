Delete Your BillFect Account

You can request deletion of your BillFect account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to:

privacy@billfect.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your BillFect account and use:

Subject: Delete my BillFect account

We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request.

What will be deleted

  • Your BillFect account
  • Account profile information
  • Usage and entitlement data associated with your account
  • Stored application data associated with your account

Data stored locally on your device can also be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling BillFect.

Data that may be retained

Certain records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

Any retained data will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
