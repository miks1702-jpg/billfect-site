BillFect Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

BillFect (“BillFect,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information BillFect collects, how we use it, how it may be processed by service providers, how long it may be retained, and the choices available to you.

BillFect allows users to record meal items and quantities, photograph a receipt, and compare the recorded items and quantities with the receipt.

1. Information We Collect

Account and Authentication Information

BillFect uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we may process information provided through Google and Firebase, including a unique Firebase user identifier, your email address, basic account information, and authentication information needed to verify your session.

BillFect does not receive or store your Google account password.

Meal Information

When you use BillFect, you may provide names of food, drinks, or other meal items, quantities, and information entered manually or through voice input. This information is used to provide BillFect's functionality.

Receipt Images

When you request a receipt comparison, the receipt image you provide is transmitted to BillFect's backend for processing.

The receipt image and recorded meal information may be processed by an artificial-intelligence service provider in order to perform the requested comparison.

BillFect does not use receipt images for advertising and does not intentionally store receipt image files in its long-term account database after processing is completed.

Voice Input

If you use voice input, microphone input may be processed by speech-recognition services available on your device or platform. BillFect uses the resulting text to help create meal items and does not intentionally store raw voice recordings on its backend.

Purchase and Entitlement Information

If you purchase a BillFect package through Google Play, BillFect may process and retain information necessary to verify and manage the purchase, including the product identifier, purchase status, purchase completion date, entitlement status, usage information, and cryptographically protected identifiers used to prevent duplicate or unauthorized use.

Payment-card information and payment credentials are handled by Google Play. BillFect does not receive your full payment-card details.

Technical and Operational Information

BillFect may process limited technical information required to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service, including request status, processing time, cache status, application or backend version information, sanitized error categories, and usage or quota information.

Our production logging is designed to avoid recording receipt contents, meal-item text, authentication tokens, passwords, purchase tokens, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Provide receipt-comparison functionality
  • Compare recorded items and quantities with receipt information
  • Display comparison results
  • Maintain recent meal history
  • Manage usage limits and entitlements
  • Verify and restore purchases
  • Prevent duplicate processing and unauthorized use
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain reliability
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell personal information and we do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing

BillFect uses an external artificial-intelligence service to assist with receipt interpretation and comparison.

Information sent for this purpose may include receipt image content, recorded meal-item names, recorded quantities, and information necessary to produce the requested comparison.

4. Service Providers

BillFect relies on third-party service providers, including:

  • Google and Firebase — authentication and related services
  • Google Cloud — backend hosting, database, security and infrastructure
  • OpenAI — AI-assisted receipt interpretation and comparison
  • Google Play — application distribution, purchases, payment processing and purchase verification

These providers may process information on our behalf according to their applicable terms, privacy practices, and our service configuration.

5. Data Storage and Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, protect users, maintain entitlement integrity, prevent fraud or duplicate processing, and satisfy legal or accounting obligations.

BillFect currently limits completed meal history to the three most recent completed meals.

Certain successful comparison replay information may expire approximately 24 hours after creation and may be deleted asynchronously thereafter.

Some minimal transaction, entitlement, anti-duplication, security, accounting, and audit records may be retained for longer when necessary to preserve the integrity of the service or comply with applicable obligations.

6. Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by BillFect, including HTTPS encryption, Firebase Authentication, restricted service identities, least-privilege permissions, protected secret management, server-side purchase verification, and sanitized production logging.

No system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to minimize unnecessary access, storage, and exposure of user information.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your BillFect account and associated personal data by contacting us at:

privacy@billfect.com

When a valid deletion request is completed, BillFect will delete or anonymize personal data associated with the account, except for limited information that must be retained for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, security, purchase disputes, accounting, legal obligations, or prevention of duplicate purchase or entitlement abuse.

8. Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion of your information.

Privacy requests may be submitted to privacy@billfect.com.

9. Children's Privacy

BillFect is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children except where permitted by applicable law and Google Play requirements.

10. International Processing

BillFect uses cloud and technology service providers that may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BillFect develops or as our legal, technical, or operational practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
