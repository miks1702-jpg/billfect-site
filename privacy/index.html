BillFect Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

BillFect (“BillFect,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be processed when you use BillFect, how that information is used, how it may be processed by service providers, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

Account and Authentication Information

BillFect may use third-party authentication services, including Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we may process information necessary to identify and authenticate your account, such as a unique user identifier, email address, and basic account information.

BillFect does not receive or store your Google account password.

Information You Provide

When using BillFect, you may choose to provide text, images, quantities, preferences, or other information required to use features of the application. This information is processed in order to provide the functionality you request.

Images and Submitted Content

Images or other content that you submit may be securely transmitted to BillFect's backend and processed in order to provide the requested application functionality.

Certain submitted content may also be processed by third-party technology or artificial-intelligence service providers acting on our behalf.

BillFect does not use submitted images or content for targeted advertising and does not intentionally retain submitted image files longer than reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality.

Voice Input

If you choose to use voice input, speech-recognition services available on your device or platform may process microphone input. BillFect may use the resulting recognized text as information you provide to the application.

BillFect does not intentionally store raw voice recordings on its backend.

Purchase and Entitlement Information

If you make a purchase through an application store, BillFect may process information necessary to verify and manage that purchase. This may include product identifiers, purchase status, purchase timestamps, entitlement status, usage information, and protected identifiers used for security and anti-duplication purposes.

Payment-card information and payment credentials are handled by the applicable application-store provider. BillFect does not receive your full payment-card details.

Technical Information

BillFect may process limited technical and operational information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of the service. This may include request status, processing times, application versions, sanitized error categories, usage information, and security-related events.

Our logging practices are designed to avoid unnecessarily recording sensitive user content, authentication credentials, passwords, payment information, or purchase credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain account access
  • Provide application functionality requested by the user
  • Maintain limited account and activity history
  • Manage usage rights and entitlements
  • Verify and manage application-store purchases
  • Prevent fraud, duplicate processing, and unauthorized use
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service
  • Diagnose technical issues and maintain reliability
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell personal information and we do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

3. Automated and AI-Assisted Processing

BillFect may use automated or artificial-intelligence-based processing to provide certain application features.

When necessary for a requested feature, information you submit may be securely transmitted to a service provider for processing. We seek to limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the requested function.

4. Service Providers

BillFect relies on third-party service providers for functions such as authentication, cloud infrastructure, data processing, artificial-intelligence processing, application distribution, payment processing, and purchase verification.

These providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their applicable terms, privacy practices, and the configuration of our services.

5. Data Storage and Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and entitlement integrity, protect against fraud or duplicate processing, maintain security, and satisfy applicable legal or accounting obligations.

Some recent activity information may be retained to support application features. Certain temporary processing or replay-protection records may be retained for limited periods and deleted asynchronously.

Minimal transaction, entitlement, security, anti-fraud, accounting, or audit records may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary.

6. Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by BillFect. These include encrypted communications, authenticated access, restricted service permissions, secure credential management, and measures intended to minimize unnecessary storage and exposure of user data.

No system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to limit unnecessary access to and retention of personal information.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your BillFect account and associated personal data by contacting:

privacy@billfect.com

After a valid deletion request is processed, personal information associated with the account will be deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible, except for limited information that may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, transaction disputes, accounting, legal obligations, or protection against duplicate or unauthorized use.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion.

Privacy requests may be submitted to privacy@billfect.com.

9. Children's Privacy

BillFect is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children except where permitted by applicable law and applicable platform requirements.

10. International Processing

BillFect uses cloud and technology service providers that may process information in countries other than the country in which you live.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BillFect develops or as legal, technical, or operational practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
